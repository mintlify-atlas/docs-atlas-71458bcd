Work Time Recorder is a zero-friction time tracker that runs entirely in your browser. Click **Start** when you begin working, click **Stop** when you're done — the app records every session, totals your hours by day, week, and month, and keeps everything in `localStorage` so nothing leaves your machine unless you choose cloud sync.

## Get started in three steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app">
    Visit the [GitHub Pages live demo](https://longlong007.github.io/work-time-recorder/) or open `index.html` locally in any modern browser — Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record your first session">
    Type an optional work name (e.g. `Feature: login page`), click **▶ Start**, work, then click **⏸ Stop**. Your session appears instantly in the history panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your stats">
    Switch to the **Stats** tab on the right panel to see today's total, this week's total, and a bar chart of your recent activity.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  No account is needed for local use. Data lives in your browser's `localStorage`. Enable [CloudBase sync](/cloud/overview) only if you want cross-device access.
</Tip>
